# Premise Check

Run this checklist before presenting a proposed solution for any new skill, workflow, or system. Do not skip steps. Each question must produce a genuine answer — not a confirmation of what you already decided.

## The Checklist

### 1. State Your Core Assumption
What is the one thing your solution assumes must be true? Write it as a single sentence.

### 2. Design Without That Assumption
Build the best possible solution that does NOT rely on that assumption. Give it real effort — not a strawman.

### 3. Compare Honestly
Which is actually better? If the alternative is better, switch. If yours is better, articulate specifically why — not just "it's simpler."

### 4. Steel-Man the Opposite
Imagine someone who strongly disagrees with your approach. What's the strongest version of their argument? Does it hold?

### 5. Find the Hidden Cost
What does your solution cost that isn't obvious? Token waste, maintenance burden, fragility, dependency on you being alive, Harrison's attention/time, complexity for future-you to understand?

### 6. Ask "What Triggers This?"
Is your solution reactive (waiting/polling for something to happen) or proactive (scheduling/causing things to happen at the right time)? If reactive — is there a proactive version? Proactive is almost always better.

### 7. Explain It to a Skeptic
Describe your solution in 2 sentences to someone who'd push back hard. If it sounds like it needs defending, it probably does.

## Termination Rule

If you re-check a recommendation and the new alternative has roughly equal merit to the previous one, **stop and pick the cheaper option to test.** Analysis that costs more than the decision it informs has failed. Maximum two full passes — after that, execute the lowest-cost experiment and learn from real results instead of further theorizing.

## How to Use

Run all 7 steps. Write out the answers. If any step changes your mind, restart from step 1 with the new approach (up to the two-pass limit above). Present the final recommendation with a brief note on what changed during the check (if anything).
